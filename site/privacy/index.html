ScreenMate Privacy Policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

In short
  • You can use ScreenMate without an account. Your watchlist and history then stay on your device.
  • If you create an account, we store your email address, your watchlist and your watch history so they sync across your devices.
  • We do not use advertising, analytics or tracking, and we never sell your data.
  • You can delete your account and all its data from inside the app at any time.

Who we are

ScreenMate is an independent app for discovering movies and TV shows, watching trailers and keeping a watchlist. This policy covers the ScreenMate apps for Android, Windows and macOS.

Information we collect

DataWhenWhy
Email address and passwordOnly if you create an accountTo sign you in. Passwords are stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see them.
WatchlistWhen you save titlesTo show your saved titles. Synced to your account only if you are signed in.
Watch history (title, season and episode, time)When you open a titleTo let you pick up where you left off. Synced only if you are signed in.

We do not collect your name, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or device identifiers, and the app contains no advertising or analytics SDKs.

Information processed by services we use

To work, the app sends requests to the following services. Like any web request, these include your IP address and basic device information (such as the app or browser version).

  • Supabase: account sign-in and storage of your synced watchlist and history.
  • Cloudflare: hosts the servers that deliver movie information and app update details.
  • TMDB (The Movie Database): movie and TV information and images. Search terms you type are sent to TMDB through our server to find matching titles.
  • JustWatch (via TMDB): which streaming services offer a title in your country. Your country is estimated on your device from your language and time-zone settings; only the country code is sent.
  • YouTube (Google): plays trailers. When you play a trailer, YouTube's own privacy policy applies.
  • GitHub: hosts app updates, which the app checks for and downloads.

These services process data under their own privacy policies. We only share what is needed for each feature to work.

How we use your information

  • To provide the app's features: sign-in, syncing your watchlist and history, and showing title details.
  • To deliver app updates.

We do not use your data for advertising, profiling or selling to anyone.

Storage and security

Data sent over the internet is encrypted in transit (HTTPS). Account data is stored with Supabase and protected so that each account can only access its own data. Data on your device stays in the app's private storage.

Keeping and deleting your data

  • Signing out removes your watchlist and history from that device.
  • Deleting your account (Account → Delete account in the app) permanently removes your account, watchlist and history from our servers right away.
  • Without an account, your data exists only on your device. Uninstalling the app or clearing its data removes it.

We keep account data only as long as your account exists.

Your rights

You can view, change or delete your data in the app at any time. You can also contact us to request a copy of your data or ask any question about it, and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

ScreenMate is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app's release notes.
