Delete in the app

  1. Open ScreenMate and sign in.
  2. Tap the Account icon (the person icon) at the top right.
  3. Tap Delete account, then Delete my account.

Can't sign in?

Reset your password in the app (Account → Sign in → Forgot password), or email screenmate.yolo@gmail.com from the address you signed up with, subject "Delete my ScreenMate account". We will delete the account within 30 days, usually much sooner, and reply when it is done.

What is deleted

  • Your account, including your email address and sign-in details
  • Your watchlist
  • Your watch history

We keep none of this after deletion. Data stored only on a device (for example, if you used the app without an account) never reached our servers; uninstall the app or clear its data to remove it.
